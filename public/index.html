How does delivery work?+

Right after your payment is confirmed you receive an email with your private download link. Set your new wallpapers in seconds.

What's the difference between the packs?+

The Phone Pack has every painting composed vertically for phones. The Desktop Pack has every painting in widescreen 4K for laptops and monitors. The Complete Collection includes both — and costs less than buying them separately.

Which devices are supported?+

iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows and tablets. If it has a screen and a wallpaper setting, Glaze works on it.

Can I use them on several devices?+

Yes. Your purchase is for personal use on all of your own devices.

Which currency will I pay in?+

We detect your country and show prices in your local currency, so what you see is what you pay. You can always switch currency from the top menu.

Is the payment secure?+

Payments are processed by Stripe, one of the world's most trusted payment platforms. We never see or store your card details.
