FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Send us a message and a real person will reply.

How does delivery work?

Right after you pay, your download opens on the next page. Stripe also emails you a receipt. Setting your new wallpaper takes less than a minute.

What is the difference between the packs?

The Phone Pack has every painting composed vertically for phones. The Desktop Pack has every painting in widescreen 4K for laptops and monitors. The Complete Collection gives you both, for less than buying them separately.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows and tablets. If your device lets you change the wallpaper, Glaze works on it.

Can I use them on more than one device?

Yes. Use them on every device you own. The files are for personal use, so please do not resell or share them.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once and the files are yours to keep. There is nothing to cancel.

Which currency will I pay in?

We detect where you are and show the price in your local currency, so what you see is what you pay. You can switch currency anytime from the menu at the top.

Is the payment secure?

Payments are handled by Stripe, the same system used by millions of online stores. We never see or store your card details.
